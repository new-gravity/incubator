Accessibility

New Gravity is committed to making this site usable by everyone. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

Our application form

Our application form is provided by Airtable, a third-party service. We have tested it, but we cannot guarantee that every part meets WCAG 2.1 AA. If you have any difficulty, contact us and we will do our best to help.

Contact us

If you have trouble using this site or the application form, email incubator@newgravity.center and we will do our best to help. We aim to respond within two business days.
