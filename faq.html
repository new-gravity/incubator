Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email us at incubator@newgravity.center.

Who should apply to the incubator?

People with the drive and skill to build a new project that changes what’s possible, and who are ready to make it their full-time work by fall 2027. Any theory of change or issue area is welcomed, so long as radical success would fundamentally alter the nature of American politics in 10-20 years. We select on talent first and the idea second.

Can I apply with an idea I’m already working on?

Some applicants will come in with a project they’ve already started piloting, but have remaining questions or hypotheses to test. Others will have a few ideas, or a problem they can’t stop thinking about.

If you’ve started something early-stage, apply and tell us what you’ve tested, learned, and share your outstanding design questions or hypotheses. If you lead an established organization, or are looking for funding for a project that is already fully formed, this isn’t the right fit for you. Instead, email incubator@newgravity.center with a 1-page pitch to be considered in our next strategic capacity funding round.

Can I apply with multiple ideas?

Yes. You don’t need an idea you’re 100% committed to. The ideation month exists to sharpen your idea, and it can change along the way. If selected for the ideation grant, you will receive virtual coaching, and you will need to arrive at Pitch Day in NYC with a single, sharp idea that you could take to the next stage.

What is New Gravity?

New Gravity is a new model of philanthropy. We use capital to build the long-term infrastructure required to win a world where everyone is fed, sheltered, and cared for. (1) We identify and develop business leaders and ultra-high-net-wealth individuals into one collective of strategic funders. (2) We back bold experiments and interventions earlier than other funders would to build a robust and evolving ecosystem that can win.

The incubator is our next step: finding and funding the people who will build the campaigns and institutions that will shape our country’s next 50 years. You can read the critique of philanthropy behind the project here.

How does the incubator work, and when?

Our incubator operates in four stages:

  1. Apply (by Jan 15). Written application, then rolling interviews in January and February.
  2. Ideation. At least 15 applicants get $3k for one month to develop their idea, with virtual coaching sessions. This stage begins in early February and ends with an in-person pitch day in NYC on March 13.
  3. Design & Beta-testing. After pitch day, we will invite those whose pitches demonstrate a clear need, a testable plan, and the commitment to pursue it to join the Founders Cohort. Cohort members will receive $10k - $40k to further design and test their idea, and will meet in person and virtually for peer support, training, and coaching. Beta-tests will vary to suit your idea, and can include developing proto-organizations, events, or projects.
  4. Launch. Founders and teams whose ideas demonstrate real traction will move to launch and receive $200k - $1M+/year for up to 2 years, plus ongoing coaching and operations support. We anticipate supporting 4-6 new founders or founding teams at this stage. To accommodate projects moving at different speeds, prospective founders will have the opportunity to pitch for launch funding in either early June or mid-September.

We cover travel costs for all in-person events.

How will you decide who “passes” at each stage?

Initial applicants will be selected primarily on talent. From there, each stage will build toward a test of your strategy. We will work with you to choose a method that makes sense for the context of your project. The goal is not to eliminate participants or create competition between them. The test to advance to the next stage exists to support effective and rigorous development. We’re looking for a clear need that the idea can address, a sharp strategy that shapes your approach and actions, and the commitment and talent to drive the project forward. We will not have a quota at any stage: we want as many projects to move forward as possible.

I already have a team. Can we all join the incubator?

This program is designed around a peer network of founders. If you have a co-founder, you may apply together, but full teams cannot apply. If you would like to apply as three co-founders, tell us why the third person is essential and we’ll consider it.

If you’re applying alone, we may ask whether you’re open to meeting other applicants as potential co-founders. Some of the strongest teams may form during the process from within the Incubator.

We believe strong teams are critical to a project’s success. Team formation and leadership will be a focus of support and coaching for the new projects as they move through the incubator.

What support will the prospective founders receive in addition to funding?

Coaching. Tailored coaching from a combination of movement veterans and private-sector founders.

A cohort of peers. Other founders taking big risks at the same time, supporting each other with internal cohort learning and feedback, plus input from trusted movement leaders and experts.

Training and shared analysis. A cohort curriculum covering startup best practices, hegemony, narrative, organizational structure, leadership, culture, and launching. As the inaugural cohort of the incubator, this Founders Cohort will collaborate to deepen our understanding of the gaps in the social change ecosystem, and the pathway forward to win.

Operations. Fiscal sponsorship and back-office support, so you can focus on the work instead of payroll and compliance.

Will the incubator run again in the future?

Yes. While we expect big things from our inaugural cohort, we see this as a multi-decade project with multiple rounds. We expect the alumni network of the incubator to grow, learn, and continue to support new founders to cycle through the incubator.
