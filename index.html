Applications close January 15, 2027

We’re living through a historical crossroads of democracy decline, climate crisis, and economic destabilization. And yet, social change and movement leaders often spend years wading through slow-moving, risk-averse organizations that fail to meet the moment. That’s why New Gravity is building a community of founders and investors willing to take big risks and try new things.

We’re working toward a world where all people are fed, sheltered, and cared for. Our Incubator is matching talented organizers and operators with funding and support to build the social movements, campaigns, institutions, and infrastructure that we need to win.

Here’s how it works

  1. Ideation
    $3K–$5K

    We will award at least 15 applicants with ideation grants of $3-5k for one month to sharpen their idea. After that, ideators will pitch our team in New York City.*

  2. Founders Cohort
    $10K–$40K

    From there, we will invite those whose pitches demonstrate a clear need, a testable plan, and the commitment to pursue it to join the Founders Cohort. Cohort members will receive peer support, coaching, training and $10k - $40k to test their ideas by developing proto-organizations, events, or projects.

  3. Launch
    $200K–$1M+/yr

    Founders whose ideas demonstrate real traction will move to the launch stage and receive $200k - $1M+/year for up to 2 years, plus ongoing coaching and operations support. We anticipate supporting 4-6 new founding teams at this stage.

*New Gravity will cover all travel and accommodation to NYC. Individuals will receive ideation grants of $3k, and pairs will receive grants of $5k.

We’re building this incubator because the social change ecosystem desperately needs fast-moving innovation. Yet, there is little funding for experimental projects, especially those that are risky and cutting edge. New Gravity exists to make big, bold bets.
